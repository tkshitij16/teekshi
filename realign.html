Your Dopamine Mapping Exercise

Ten quiet minutes. No algorithm. Just you finding your way back to yourself.

1

Silence the Feed

Phone face-down. Every tab closed. Sit for three minutes with zero input. Notice what surfaces naturally — those spontaneous thoughts are breadcrumbs back to your real self.

2

Name Your Last 5 Genuine Smiles

Not from a reel or a meme. From real life — a conversation, a meal, a walk, a moment of creation. Write them down. Look for the pattern. That pattern is your signal.

3

The Ancestor Question

If you lived in 1850 — no internet, no algorithm, no infinite scroll — what would you spend your days doing? Your answer reveals your core nature: the human beneath the user.

4

Define the One Thing

What skill, craft, relationship, or pursuit would feel deeply meaningful even if no one ever witnessed it? That is your compass. Not your career. Not your content. You. Start there today.

5

Write the Promise

Finish this sentence by hand, on paper: "I feel most alive when..." — read it back to yourself. That sentence is worth more than any algorithm's recommendation about who you should be.

The Condition

What happens when algorithms know you better than you know yourself?

For hundreds of thousands of years, humans found meaning through survival, craft, community, and creation. Then — in a single generation — the rules changed. AI thinks for us. Algorithms choose our entertainment. Machines compete for our jobs, our attention, and even our sense of identity.

The question our generation faces is no longer "how do I succeed?" It is something deeper: "what does it even mean to be human when machines can do almost everything?"

Curiosity — the original human superpower — is the one force AI cannot replicate. Curiosity about life itself. What will make you happy in 2035? What will satisfaction feel like when creation is instant and effortless? These are not questions a model can answer for you. They are yours alone.

This page is not a solution. It is an invitation to stop outsourcing your inner life and start navigating it again.

The Three Great Questions of Our Era

Curiosity is the compass.
These are its cardinal directions.

Every generation faced a disruption that forced humanity to redefine what a good life looks like. This one is ours.

🧠

What will make people happy?

When entertainment is infinite, beauty is filtered, and connection is mediated — what does genuine joy actually feel like from the inside? Happiness must be rediscovered, not downloaded.

⚗️

What will satisfaction look like?

When AI produces in seconds what took humans a lifetime to master, where does pride of craft go? Satisfaction must be relocated — from output to process, from product to personhood.

🔮

How will life feel in the AI age?

We are the first generation writing this script, not inheriting one. The curious among us — not the most optimised, but the most alive — will define what it means to be human in this era.
